Yuji Itadori
Vessel of Sukuna

A boy with immense physical strength who consumes a finger of Sukuna, the King of Curses, and becomes his vessel to save his friends.

Gojo Satoru
The Honored One

Widely recognized as the strongest sorcerer in the world. Holder of both the Limitless and Six Eyes techniques.

Ryomen Sukuna
King of Curses

The main antagonist of the Jujutsu Kaisen series, he was history's strongest jujutsu sorcerer.

Yuta Okkotsu
Special Grade

The protagonist of JJK 0. He carries the powerful curse of his childhood friend Rika and possesses immense cursed energy.

Fushiguro Megumi
Ten Shadows User

A first-year student at Jujutsu High and a descendant of the Zenin family, utilizing shadows to summon powerful shikigami.

Zenin Maki
Heavenly Restriction

Born with low cursed energy but extraordinary physical prowess, she seeks to become a master sorcerer despite her family's rejection.

Geto Suguru
Curse Manipulation

A former friend of Gojo turned into the worst curse user. He seeks a world where only sorcerers exist.

Nobara Kugisaki
Grade 3 sorcerer

Nobara is ranked as a grade 3 jujutsu sorcerer by Jujutsu High, she had a solid understanding of how to exorcise curses with her innate technique.
